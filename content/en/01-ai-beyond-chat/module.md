# Module 1: AI beyond chat

## Goal

By the end of this module, you will understand:
- how a language model generates text and code;
- why AI sometimes makes things up, and how to check;
- what context is and why AI "forgets";
- the difference between a chat and an agent that works on your files.

And you will have created your first artifact with AI: a web page of your own, kept in your repository.

Still nothing to install: everything from a browser or a phone.
