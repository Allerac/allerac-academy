# Module 0: First day at work

## Goal

By the end of this module, you will have:
- used AI to solve a real problem;
- created your GitHub account;
- created your first repository and made your first commit;
- opened your first issue in a real Allerac project.

All from a browser or a phone, with nothing to install.
