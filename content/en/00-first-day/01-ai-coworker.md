# 0.1 AI is your coworker

## Problem

A passenger missed their connecting flight and sent a complaint in English. You need to reply politely and clearly, in 2 minutes. How would you do it?

*Try to think of a reply before you go on.*

## Blocks

### 1. What a prompt is
A prompt is the request you give the AI. It is like handing a task to a coworker: the clearer the request, the better the result.

> **Tutor notes:** compare it to giving instructions to a new coworker who is smart but knows nothing about the context. Common mistake: thinking the AI "guesses" what the person wants.

### 2. Context changes everything
"Write an email" gets you a generic email. "Write a polite reply in English to a passenger who missed their connection because of a flight delay, apologizing and explaining the rebooking options" gets you exactly what you need.

Good context answers: **who**, **what**, **for whom**, **in what tone** and **with what goal**.

> **Tutor notes:** ask the student to rewrite a weak prompt by adding context. Show the difference side by side.

### 3. You can ask for improvements
The first answer is rarely the final one. You can ask: "shorter", "more formal", "don't apologize so many times". Talking to the AI is a back and forth.

> **Tutor notes:** reinforce that asking for changes is normal and not a "failure". Common mistake: accepting the first answer, or starting over instead of asking for a change.

### 4. You are responsible for the result
AI can make mistakes or make things up. Read and check before you use it. You are the one sending the email.

> **Tutor notes:** do not go deep here; module 1 covers hallucinations. Just plant the habit of always reviewing.
